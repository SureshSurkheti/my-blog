A Little About Me

I'm a full-stack developer, and I've been working as a software engineer since 2022 — the last three years at an IT company here in Japan.

Outside of work, I travel. Japan has a way of putting an onsen town, a mountain trail and a quiet shrine within a few hours of each other, and I'd rather see those places than read about them.

This blog is where the two sides meet: the places I go, the photos I take, and what I learn along the way — some of it about code, most of it not.
